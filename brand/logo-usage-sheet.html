Ciao Italian Taxi Service · Logo usage

Gear (a tyre with tread teeth), five spokes, the Stella d'Italia as the hub, and a red ribbon carrying the name. All lettering is outlined: no font is needed to reproduce it.

Clear space · equal to the ribbon's depth

R

Keep at least R (the depth of the red band) clear on every side. Nothing, including other text, enters that zone.

Do not

  • Add gradients, drop shadows, bevels or outlines.
  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or re-letter the mark.
  • Use the lettered emblem below 150px wide.
  • Add olive or oak branches or "Repubblica Italiana": it is not the state emblem.
